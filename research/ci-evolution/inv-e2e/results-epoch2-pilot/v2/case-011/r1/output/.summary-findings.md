## Findings

- **MC-1 — Pre-Vote responses of an earlier round are accepted by a later round of the same term** — Status: `REPRODUCED`. Impact: The grant is tallied by the wrong round: RaftCore panics (Fatal::Panicked) when the responder is no longer a voter, and otherwise a real election can start on grants the current round never received, inflating the term..
- **MC-2 — A Pre-Vote round started while Candidate survives leader establishment; the new Leader starts another election** — Status: `REPRODUCED`. Impact: The freshly established Leader steps down and starts an election at term+1 (term inflation, leadership loss); in debug builds RaftCore panics on try_leader_handler's assertion leader.vote >= state.vote..
- Other dispositions: 1.
