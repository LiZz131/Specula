## Findings

- **MC-1 — NEWLEADER ACK is sent before the DIFF-synced transactions are durable** — Status: `REPRODUCED`. Impact: A new leader is elected without the transaction that clients already read, and the old leader's only copy is truncated when it rejoins (lost committed write)..
- **CR-2 — Learner failure between NEWLEADER and UPTODATE leaks an INITIAL ZooKeeperServer** — Status: `REPRODUCED`. Impact: Each failed sync leaks the abandoned server's processor threads (3 failures left 3 extra CommitProcessor threads; none on the old revision); the leaked SyncThread keeps writing the shared log after the peer left the session and the peer's election vote uses a data tree older than its durable log (observed in a real trace)..
- **CR-3 — A proposal ACK can be consumed as the NEWLEADER ACK** — Status: `REPRODUCED`. Impact: The leader misses its NEWLEADER quorum although every learner acked; activation waited for a learner sync timeout and re-sync (about 22 s versus under 4 s on the old revision)..
