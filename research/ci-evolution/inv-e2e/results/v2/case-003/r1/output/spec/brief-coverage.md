## Non-vacuity

20 canaries (`MC_reach_*.cfg`, `spec/output/reachability-summary.log`) each
produced a witness: the 7 V0 markers plus accepted rows 1/3/5/7/9, rejected
basic/rows 4/6/8, the unchanged-value rejection, an enabled stored block, and
an enable-height update crossing mock and real replay. Trace mutations
(`spec/output/trace-mutation-controls.log`) show that Trace.tla consumes the new
fields. `Update_discharge_control.cfg` shows that the discharge check can fail.

## Intentional exclusions and limits

As V0 (no peers/voting/evidence/transactions/validator updates/pruning/state
sync; no I/O fault injection). Extended-commit storage itself is not observed in
traces (`veHist` is read from the state-store params history). InitChain may set
the enable height without `ValidateUpdate` (replay.go:346-347); unchanged by the
update and not modeled. Bounds per cfg; results are LIMITED_COVERAGE.
